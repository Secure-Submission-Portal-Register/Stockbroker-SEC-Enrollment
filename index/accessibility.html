Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to making this website usable by everyone, including people with disabilities.

Last updated: January 2026

Our Commitment

The Securities and Exchange Commission is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA and Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act.

Accessibility Features

  • Semantic HTML structure for compatibility with screen readers.
  • Keyboard-navigable forms, menus, and interactive elements.
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and background elements.
  • Descriptive labels for all form fields and buttons.
  • Responsive design that adapts to assistive zoom and mobile devices.

Ongoing Improvements

We regularly review our website for accessibility barriers and work to resolve issues as they are identified. This is an ongoing effort as we continue to improve usability for all visitors.

Feedback & Assistance

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of this website, please contact our Accessibility Coordinator at accessibility@sec.gov or call 1-800-555-0192. Please describe the issue and the page where it occurred so we can assist you promptly.
