Freedom of Information Act (FOIA)

How to request records from the Securities and Exchange Commission under FOIA.

About FOIA

The Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) gives the public the right to request access to records from federal agencies. The SEC is committed to transparency and processes FOIA requests in accordance with federal law.

How to Submit a FOIA Request

  1. Submit your request in writing by email, mail, or through the Secure Upload Portal (select "Other Supporting Document" as the document type).
  2. Clearly describe the records you are seeking with as much detail as possible.
  3. Include your name and contact information so we can respond to your request.
  4. Indicate whether you are requesting a fee waiver and the basis for the request, if applicable.
