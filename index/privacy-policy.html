Privacy Policy

How the SEC collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: January 2026

Information We Collect

When you use the Secure Upload Portal, we collect the information you voluntarily provide, including your full name, date of birth, contact information, and any documents you choose to submit. We may also collect limited technical information, such as browser type, for security and site performance purposes.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and review regulatory document submissions.
  • To communicate with you regarding the status of your submission.
  • To maintain records required under applicable federal retention schedules.
  • To improve the security and functionality of our website.

Data Security

All submissions are encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols. Access to submitted documents is restricted to authorized personnel for the purpose of compliance review.

Information Sharing

The SEC does not sell or rent personal information. Information may be shared with other federal or state regulatory agencies when required by law or as part of an active investigation.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable record-retention requirements, by contacting privacy@sec.gov.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy may be directed to our Privacy Office at privacy@sec.gov or through our Contact page.
